## Design Direction
- **Dark by default** — `#0d0d0d` / `#111111` bg
- Accent: electric blue (`#3b82f6`) or cyan (`#06b6d4`)
- Typography: Inter or Space Grotesk for headings
- Clean cards, confident whitespace, brutalist touches (bold labels, strong dividers)
- NOT: terminal cosplay, pastel gradients, excessive animation
- Target audience: defense contractor hiring manager + startup CTO
